# Third-party credits

The phone sample uses original caller names, character descriptions, greetings and reference recordings from *Scam With Your Friends*, Jatater Worldwide LLC. The supplied voice recordings come from the game's Connor collection.

- **Whisper.Unity** by Macoron binds **whisper.cpp** by the ggml authors and **Whisper** by OpenAI. All three MIT texts are in `Whisper.md`. Optional model downloads use GGML conversions from ggerganov’s repository. Keep the notices with redistributed models and binaries. [Whisper.Unity](https://github.com/Macoron/whisper.unity) · [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) · [OpenAI Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper)
- **Pocket TTS Unity** by Bakhtiar Lukman (lookbe) is MIT licensed. The optional adapter includes the license and Jatater’s modified game runtime with bounded audio frames, transcript timing and the ONNX arena fix. The Windows SentencePiece wrapper uses Google's Apache 2.0 SentencePiece. Preserve the included Apache license and the WDL/NAudio resampler’s notices in source. [Unity runtime](https://github.com/lookbe/pocket-tts-unity) · [SentencePiece](https://github.com/google/sentencepiece)
